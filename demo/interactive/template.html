Shot List Coverage

A client sends a shot list. After the event you have a card full of frames. This Skill checks the list against the shoot folder on the NAS and says, line by line, which shots are covered, weak, or missing.

    The client's shot list

    The Rivera-Okoro engagement party. shots, of them must-have. The Skill keeps the client's words and the order of the list.

    rivera-okoro-shot-list.pdf · fictional sample

    The request

    You copy the card to the NAS, then ask in plain words. The Skill reads the list and shows it back to you. It continues only after you confirm.

    You · expected flowCheck my shot list Documents/rivera-okoro-shot-list.pdf against Photos/2026-11 Rivera-Okoro party. The event is the Rivera-Okoro engagement party. Ana is Ana Rivera, Daniel is Daniel Okoro, Grandma Rosa is Rosa Rivera, and Maya is Maya Chen.
    Skill · expected flowThe shoot folder has images. I read shots from the list. The must-have shots are . Is this list correct?
    #ShotMustPeople
    Confirmed. The Skill starts the check.

    How each line is checked

    First a search of the shoot folder, built from the line itself. Then the NAS vision model looks at the best candidates and answers four questions about each one. Pick a shot to see its search and the frames the demo shoot holds for it.


    1. Search the shoot folder

    2. Vision check of each candidate

    1. Does the photo show the shot? yes, partly, or no
    2. Is the main subject in sharp focus?
    3. Are the eyes of the main people open?
    4. Is a person or object the shot needs cut off by the edge?
    StatusRule
    coveredAt least one good frame: shows the shot, sharp, eyes open, nothing cut off.
    weakNo good frame, but at least one photo shows the shot or part of it.
    missingNo photo shows the shot, or the search found no candidates.

    The report

    Missing must-have shots come first, then the counts, then every shot in list order. Select a row to see the frames for that shot.

    The closed-eyes case

    Shot 10, Ana with Grandma Rosa, is a must-have. The demo shoot has six frames of it. In three of them, eyes are closed. Take the three eyes-open frames out of the folder and run the check again.

    Frames of shot 10 in the folder

    A frame with closed eyes still shows the shot, so the shot is not missing. But it is not a good frame, so the shot is weak, not covered, and it goes to the top of the report. You know before you leave the venue.

    Approve, then album and report

    The Skill only reads photos. It makes an album and saves the report only after you say so, and the NAS asks for approval before anything is written.

    Skill · expected flowMake an album with the best frames and save this report in the shoot folder? You can answer: album and report, album only, report only, or no.
