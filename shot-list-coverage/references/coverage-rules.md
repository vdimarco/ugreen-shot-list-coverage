# Coverage rules

## Read the shot list

- One item is one shot. Keep the words of the client. Do not merge two lines into one item.
- A line with "and" can name two people in one shot, for example "Bride and grandmother". Keep it as one shot that needs both people.
- A line that asks for a set, for example "Each table, wide", stays one item. Its notes give the number of matching photos.
- Must-have: a line with an asterisk, the word "must", or a line under a heading such as "Must-have" or "Do not miss".
- Section headings such as "Getting ready", "Ceremony", or "Reception" give the part of the day. Use them with the time windows from the user.

## Search cues for each shot

Build the search for step 3 from these cues, in this order:

1. **People**: named people in the photo app. Use the names that the user gave for each role. If a role has no name, search without people and say so in the notes.
2. **Time window**: the time window of the section. A shot in "Ceremony" is searched in the ceremony window first.
3. **Objects**: things that the line names, for example rings, cake, flowers, car, or kitchen.
4. **Visible text**: text that must be in the photo, for example a sign, a menu, or a house number.
5. **Scene keyword**: the shot text in a few plain words, for example "first kiss", "family group outside", or "wide room view".

## Status of a shot

Use only the answers from step 4. A good frame is a photo with these answers: Question 1 "yes", Question 2 "yes", Question 3 "yes" or "no faces", and Question 4 "no".

| Status | Rule |
|---|---|
| covered | At least one good frame. |
| weak | No good frame, but at least one photo with Question 1 "yes" or "partly". |
| missing | No photo with Question 1 "yes" or "partly", or no candidates in step 3. |
| not checked | The run stopped before step 4 checked this shot, or the step failed. |

For a "weak" shot, write the reason in the notes, for example "eyes closed in all 3 matches", "out of focus", "grandmother cut off at the edge", or "only a partial match".

## Best frame and alternates

1. Use the good frames first, then the photos with Question 1 "yes", then the photos with Question 1 "partly".
2. In the same group, use the photo that the search ranked higher in step 3.
3. The best frame is the first photo. The alternates are the next 2 photos.
4. Never use one photo as the best frame for two shots, unless the shot list asks for the same moment twice. If two shots have the same best frame, keep it for the shot with the higher rank in that photo, and use the next photo for the other shot.

## Limits

- At most 60 shots in one run.
- At most 8 candidates for each shot in step 3, and at most 6 checked in step 4.
- At most 150 photos checked in step 4 in one run. Check the must-have shots first.
- Search only in the shoot folder.
